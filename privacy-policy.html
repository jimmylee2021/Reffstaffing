Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website and services, we may collect the following types of information:

A. Information Provided Voluntarily by You

  • Contact & Inquiry Forms: When you use our "Send us a message" or "Get in Touch" features, we collect your Name, Email Address, and the contents of Your Message.
  • Job Applicants & Candidates: If you engage with our "Join Our Team" or "Careers" sections, we may collect professional and recruitment-related information necessary to evaluate your application.

B. Compliance & Safeguarding Data

    • As a specialist healthcare and social care staffing provider, we have strict statutory and regulatory obligations. For candidates and deployed staff, we collect and process background verification data including, but not limited to:
  • Identity documents and Right to Work verification
  • Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check results
  • Employment history and professional Reference Checks
  • Mandatory training, qualifications, and safeguarding awareness records

C. Automatically Collected Data

  • Cookies and Analytics: We may collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and operating system to optimize website performance and user experience.

How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • To Fulfill a Contract / Take Pre-Contractual Steps: To respond to your inquiries, provide tailored staffing solutions to healthcare or social care organizations, or evaluate your suitability for employment.
  • Legal Obligations: To carry out mandatory safeguarding compliance, including verification of professional credentials, Right to Work, and DBS background checks required for health and social care sectors.
  • Legitimate Interests: To maintain website security, improve our support services, and communicate updates relevant to your inquiry.

How We Share Your Data

We do not sell your data. We only share your personal information under strict confidentiality terms in the following scenarios:

  • With Healthcare and Social Care Clients: If you are a candidate, your profile, training verification, compliance status, and relevant professional details will be shared with prospective care settings seeking staffing solutions.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: With trusted vendors who support our business infrastructure (e.g., website hosting, secure IT systems, background check processors).
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law, regulation, or regulatory bodies monitoring UK care standards and safeguarding compliance.

Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, altered, or accessed in an unauthorized way. Access to sensitive safeguarding and recruitment records is strictly restricted to authorized staff involved in compliance monitoring.

Data Retention

We will only retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes we collected it for, including satisfying any legal, accounting, or regulatory reporting requirements.

Your Legal Rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • The right to access copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • The right to erasure ("the right to be forgotten") under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict or object to the processing of your data.
  • The right to data portability.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@refstaffingltd.co.uk. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority for data protection issues.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our operational practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted directly on this page.
